## 3. Google Business Profile (do first, it's the one that matters)

Searches like "security agency near me" (28.5K a month) are answered from Google Maps, not from web pages.

1. Go to **business.google.com**, sign in with the company Google account (the one that owns Search Console is best), then **Add business**.
2. **Name:** `20fourr`. **Category:** primary **Security guard service**. Add **Security service** and **Bodyguard service** as additional categories if Google offers them.
3. "Do you want to add a location customers can visit?" → **No**. That makes it a service-area business: Google uses the Dehradun address for verification and does not show it.
4. **Service areas** (up to 20). Use the cities that have guide pages: Mumbai, Pune, Delhi, Hyderabad, Bengaluru, Chennai, Ahmedabad, Surat, Jaipur, Gurugram, Noida, Lucknow, Kolkata, Chandigarh, Dehradun, Indore, Kochi.
5. **Phone** +91 92595 77593. **Website:** `https://20fourr.com/?utm_source=google&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=gbp`. The site's canonical tag strips the tracking part for search, and Analytics can then tell Maps clicks apart.
6. **Verify:** Google will ask for a video of the office, a postcard to the Dehradun address, or a phone call. Usually video now: show the signage or name board, the street, and that you can open the office.
7. After verification:
   - paste the long description and the services list
   - set hours (if you take bookings round the clock, say so; otherwise give your support hours)
   - upload the logo (export `public/logo-mark.svg` as a 720×720 PNG) and `public/og.png` as the cover
   - add a few app screenshots
8. **Reviews:** ask real clients with completed bookings to review. Never offer anything in return, and never review it yourselves; Google removes those and can suspend the profile.
